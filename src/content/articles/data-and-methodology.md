The atlas combines structured heritage-place records with geographic coordinates and selected signals from the Wikimedia ecosystem.

## Data sources

- [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/) supplies structured identifiers and descriptive fields.
- [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/) provides article context and popularity signals where available.
- [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) hosts many of the images shown in place records.
- [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) provides the map tiles and geographic context.

## Local processing

The downloadable dataset is stored in the browser and queried locally. This keeps catalogue exploration responsive and reduces repeat network traffic. The application checks GitHub Releases for newer dataset versions and verifies downloaded archives before installing them.

## Limits and corrections

Coverage reflects the completeness and modelling choices of the source data. Coordinates, classifications, dates, translations, and popularity measures may be missing or imperfect. Confirm important claims against the linked source records.
